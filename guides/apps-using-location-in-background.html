Which apps use your location in the background on Android?

“Allowed all the time” means an app can know where you are even when you're not using it. Here's how to find those apps.

Updated 17 October 2026

Since Android 10, location access comes in levels. Most apps only need your location while you're using them. A few – navigation, fitness trackers, family-locator apps – may have a reason to use it in the background. Everything else deserves a second look.

The levels of location access

SettingWhat the app can doTypical use
Allow all the timeUse your location at any moment, also when the app is closed.Navigation during a trip, step and run tracking, locating a family member.
Allow only while using the appUse your location only while the app is open on screen (or shows a visible notification).Weather, maps, taxi and delivery apps.
Ask every timeAsk again each time the app wants your location.Apps you rarely use.
Don't allowNo location at all.Games, flashlights, most shopping apps.

Android 12 and later also lets you choose between a precise and an approximate location. Approximate is enough for weather and local news.

Find apps that use your location all the time

  1. Open Settings › Location.
  2. Tap App location permissions (on some phones: App permissions).
  3. The first list, Allowed all the time, is the one to check.
  4. Tap an app and choose Allow only while using the app or Don't allow if it doesn't need more.

Tip: Settings › Security & privacy › Privacy › Privacy dashboard shows which apps actually used your location in the last 24 hours (on recent Android versions, the last 7 days).

Questions to ask about each app

  • Does the app do something for me while I'm not using it – navigation, tracking a workout, sharing my location with family?
  • Do I still use the app at all? Unused apps can simply be removed.
  • Did I give this access on purpose, or just tap “Allow” during setup?
  • A game, flashlight, wallpaper or shopping app with location “all the time”.
  • An app that started asking for location after an update, without a new feature that explains it.

Android helps a little – but not with history

Android shows a location icon in the status bar while an app uses your location, and it removes permissions from apps you haven't used for a few months. What it doesn't show is when an app got location access, or which update made an app start asking for it.

Frequently asked questions

What does “Allow all the time” mean for location?

The app may use your location whenever it wants, also when it's closed or running in the background. Only apps that really work in the background, such as navigation or fitness tracking, usually need it.

Does turning off background location break apps?

Most apps keep working normally with “only while using the app”. If a feature stops working, Android or the app will ask again and you can decide then.

Can an app see my location if Location is turned off?

Not through GPS-based location services. Apps can still estimate a rough area from your IP address when you're online, but that doesn't use the location permission.
