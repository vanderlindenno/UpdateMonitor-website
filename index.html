What does UpdateMonitor do?

Update history

One clear line per app, grouped by day: from which version to which, with the app's own icon and the time. Older updates fold out with one tap.

Installs & removals

New apps and removed apps are recorded too, each clearly marked.

Permission changes

A small shield shows when an update adds or removes access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts and more.

Who can access what

For each kind of access – camera, location, microphone and more – see which apps actually have it, and since when. Special access such as accessibility is listed separately.

Outside the Play Store

Apps installed from an APK file or another store are flagged with their source, with their own filter and optional notification.

Storage at a glance

Used and free space in one line, and how much it changed this week. Turns yellow when your phone is almost full. Can be hidden.

Warnings, not noise

Optional and off by default: a notification only when an app gets new permissions or is installed outside the Play Store. Ordinary updates stay quiet.

Activity calendar

A monthly heatmap shows on which days your apps changed. Tap a day to see what happened.

Search & filter

Search by app, version or date, and filter by period, type of change, source or permission changes.

Export

Share your history as a CSV spreadsheet or a PDF report – handy when IT support asks what changed.

Widget

The latest three changes right on your home screen.

100% ad-free

No ads, no tracking, no account and no analytics. Free to use.

Permissions

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES – to see all installed apps, their versions and requested permissions. This is the core of the app; the welcome screen explains it before anything is read.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – for warnings about new permissions and installs outside the Play Store. Only asked for when you turn warnings on in the app's settings. Off by default.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – to keep the hourly check scheduled after a restart.

Why permission changes matter

Most app updates are harmless. But sometimes an update quietly asks for more – your location, your contacts or your microphone. UpdateMonitor marks that right in the history with a small shield and the number of new permissions; open the app to see exactly which ones.

The Permissions tab turns it around: pick camera, location or microphone and see every app that has that access, with the newest ones marked. You can also filter the history to show only updates with permission changes, and – if you turn notifications on – get told when it happens.

Useful for IT support

"It worked yesterday – what changed?" With UpdateMonitor the answer is one tap away. Export the history as a PDF or CSV and share it with your helpdesk. Apps installed through Google Play, a company portal or another installer are shown with their source, and anything from outside the Play Store stands out.

Frequently asked questions

How can I see which apps were updated on my Android phone?

Open UpdateMonitor: it lists every update, install and removal grouped by day, with the old and new version and the time. The history is stored on your device, so you can look back at earlier updates.

How can I see which apps can use my camera, microphone or location?

The permissions overview shows, per permission, how many apps are allowed to use it and which apps they are. You can also review this in Android's own settings.

Does UpdateMonitor collect my data?

No. The app has no internet permission at all, so nothing can leave your phone. There is no account, no ads and no analytics.

How do I find apps installed outside the Play Store?

UpdateMonitor flags apps that did not come from the Play Store, lets you filter on the source, and warns you when such an app is installed.

Is UpdateMonitor free?

Yes. UpdateMonitor is free to use, with no ads and no account.
